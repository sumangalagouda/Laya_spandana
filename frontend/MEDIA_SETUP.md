# Media Setup

Place your audio files as:
- public/audio/tishra_alaripu.mp3
- public/audio/chatusra_alaripu.mp3
- public/audio/kandam_alaripu.mp3

Place your video files as:
- public/videos/tishra_dance.mp4
- public/videos/chatusra_dance.mp4
- public/videos/kandam_dance.mp4

These can be recordings you make yourself or downloaded from licensed classical music/dance archives.
